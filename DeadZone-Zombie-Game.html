HOW TO PLAY

Mouse — aim   Left click — shoot   Right click — aim down sights
W A S D — move   Shift — sprint   Space — jump

Kill zombies to earn coins. Spend them in the Armory on new guns and upgrades.
Coins you earn are kept even if you die. Progress saves automatically.

Zombies: Walker · Runner (fast) · Tank (tough) · Exploder (blows up — shoot it near others!) · Spitter (ranged acid) · Bosses
Pickups: green = health, blue = ammo, gold = coins (they fly to you).
